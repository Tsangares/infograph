# Audit: Easter Island Walking Statues (v2)

## Overall Assessment

Watchable but weak. The core problem is **image selection** — most assets don't match their scene's message. Only scene 2 (moai row) genuinely works. The writing revisions from v1 audit landed well, but good captions over wrong images still feels off.

---

## CRITICAL — Image/Content Mismatch

### Scene 3 (Ropes, 9-14s) — `moai_painting.jpg`
**Caption**: "Rocked upright and walked / with just ropes and teamwork"
**Image**: William Hodges painting of moai on an ahu (ceremonial platform). Beautiful painting but it shows statues **standing still on a platform** — the opposite of the "walking with ropes" message.
**Need**: An image showing the walking technique — people pulling ropes on a standing moai, or a diagram of the rocking method. The 2011 National Geographic experiment photos are ideal. Even a simple illustration of the three-rope rocking technique would work better.

### Scene 4 (Experiment, 14-19s) — `moai_unfinished.jpg`
**Caption**: "In 2011, 18 people / walked a 5-ton replica"
**Image**: Close-up of an unfinished moai still embedded in rock at Rano Raraku. This is a carving/quarry shot — it has nothing to do with walking or experiments.
**Need**: Photo from the actual 2011 experiment (Carl Lipo / Terry Hunt). Ideally the famous shot of the replica moai being walked by a team with ropes. This is the most important image in the video — it's the proof shot.

### Scene 5 (Map, 19-23s) — `easter_island_map.png`
**Caption**: "1 KM PER DAY" / "Rocking down ancient roads"
**Image**: A Wikipedia topographic map. It's informational but tiny (low resolution for 1080x1920), and it's a geography reference — it doesn't show moai roads, transport routes, or anything about movement.
**Need**: Either a map showing the actual moai transport routes from Rano Raraku to ahu sites around the coast (these maps exist in archaeological literature), OR skip the map entirely and use a dramatic wide shot of a moai road/path with the stat overlaid.

### Scene 6 (Close, 23-28s) — `moai_museum.jpg`
**Caption**: "The islanders always said / the statues walked. / We just didn't believe them."
**Image**: Museum moai head under fluorescent gallery lighting. Kills the drama. This is the emotional climax — it needs grandeur, not a museum exhibit.
**Need**: Moai silhouette at sunset/dusk, or the iconic Ahu Tongariki row at golden hour. Something cinematic and final.

---

## WORKING

### Scene 1 (Title, 0-4s) — `moai_hillside.jpg`
Moai heads emerging from the hillside at Rano Raraku. Good image, recognizable, ghosted behind the title text. The lighter overlay (`#00000077`) from the v1 fix should let it show through now. This works.

### Scene 2 (Moai row, 4-9s) — `moai_row.jpg`
The Ahu Tongariki row — **this is the one image that works.** Iconic, instantly recognizable, high quality. Good Ken Burns target. Text placement in lower third is solid.
